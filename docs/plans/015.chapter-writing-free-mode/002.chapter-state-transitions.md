# 002 — Open, close and reopen in the chapter service

## Goal

Add the three owner-only state transitions to the chapter service, enforcing the one-open-chapter
singleton against `open` **and** `closing`, and closing straight to `closed` with no continuity
mechanics.

## Source files

```
backend/app/services/chapters.py
```

## Test files

```
backend/tests/services/test_chapter_states.py
```

## Interface intent

**`backend/app/services/chapters.py`** — three new entry points plus two new reason members. 014's
entry points, step 001's guards and the shared private resolver and response mapper are all reused
unchanged.

- **Two new reason members** on the same enum: the chapter is not closed (a reopen precondition), and
  another chapter in the book already holds the open slot. Both map to `409` (step 003).
  The **existing not-planned reason is reused** for the open transition and the **existing not-open
  reason** (step 001's) for the close transition — the error carries `(reason, message)`, so each call
  site supplies its own message.
- **A private open-slot guard** — given the access context, load the book's chapters and raise the
  another-chapter-open reason when **any** of them is in `open` **or** `closing`. It takes the chapter
  being acted on so it can ignore that row. **The `closing` half is deliberate and is not close
  mechanics** (`context.md` → D8): a chapter parked in `closing` holds the slot.
- **`open_chapter`** — requires the **state** capability (owner only), then step 001's archived guard,
  then resolves the chapter, then refuses with the not-planned reason unless its state is `planned`,
  then runs the open-slot guard, then writes state `open` and stamps `modified_at`. Returns the
  chapter response (014's DTO, 014's mapper). It touches **state and `modified_at` only** — no body,
  no version, no ordinal, no change row, no revision row.
- **`close_chapter`** — requires the state capability, the archived guard, resolves, refuses with the
  not-open reason unless the state is `open`, then writes state **`closed`** and stamps `modified_at`.
  **It never writes `closing`, drafts nothing, and approves nothing** (`context.md` → D8 and "The
  close seam").
- **`reopen_chapter`** — requires the state capability, the archived guard, resolves, refuses with the
  not-closed reason unless the state is `closed`, runs the open-slot guard, then writes state `open`
  and stamps `modified_at`. It writes no `ChapterChange` and no `ChapterTextRevision` — a reopen moves
  no text, and the variant history a later edit produces is `018`'s, from the ordinary save path.

**No transition is collaboration-mode qualified** — all three are owner-only, and an owner is never
held for review.

## Definition of done

1. `[test]` The owner opens a `planned` chapter in a book with nothing open and its state becomes
   `open` — **US-036.AC-1**, **UC-035**.
2. `[test]` A **co-author** attempting to open a chapter is refused by the authorization error and the
   chapter's state is unchanged — **US-036.AC-2**.
3. `[test]` Opening a chapter while another chapter in the book is **`open`** is refused with the
   another-chapter-open reason, and **both** chapters keep their states — **US-037.AC-2**, **UC-035**
   exception flow.
4. `[test]` Opening a chapter while another chapter in the book is **`closing`** is refused the same
   way — **US-038.AC-4**, tested against a chapter seeded directly into `closing`.
5. `[test]` Across any sequence of open, close and reopen calls, at most one of the book's chapters is
   ever in the `open` state — **US-037.AC-1**.
6. `[test]` Opening a chapter that is `open`, `closing` or `closed` is refused with the not-planned
   reason and nothing changes.
7. `[test]` The owner closes an `open` chapter and its state becomes **`closed`** directly — it does
   **not** pass through `closing` and no summary, continuity or approval record is written —
   **US-038.AC-1** and `context.md` → "The close seam".
8. `[test]` A **co-author** attempting to close the open chapter is refused by the authorization error
   and the chapter stays `open` — **US-038.AC-2**.
9. `[test]` Closing a chapter that is `planned`, `closing` or `closed` is refused with the not-open
   reason and nothing changes.
10. `[test]` After a close, the book has no chapter in `open` or `closing`, and a different `planned`
    chapter can then be opened — the slot is genuinely released.
11. `[test]` The owner reopens a `closed` chapter when nothing else is open and its state becomes
    `open` — **US-039.AC-1**, **UC-037**.
12. `[test]` Reopening a `closed` chapter while a **different** chapter is `open` is refused with the
    another-chapter-open reason and the open chapter is unaffected — **US-039.AC-2**, CF1.
13. `[test]` Reopening while a different chapter is **`closing`** is refused the same way —
    **US-038.AC-4**.
14. `[test]` Reopening a chapter that is `planned`, `open` or `closing` is refused with the not-closed
    reason and nothing changes.
15. `[test]` All three transitions are refused with the archived reason on a book whose state is
    `archived`, and no state changes — **D10**.
16. `[test]` A **reader** and a caller with no relationship are refused all three by the authorization
    error.
17. `[test]` All three transitions touch **state and `modified_at` only**: the chapter's `text`,
    `version`, `ordinal`, `title` and `sketch` are unchanged, and no `ChapterChange` or
    `ChapterTextRevision` row is created by any of them.
18. `[test]` A chapter id belonging to **another book** is not-found on all three entry points.
