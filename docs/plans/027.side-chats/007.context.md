## Gotchas local to this step

- **Swap, do not hide.** Render one `ActionIcon` whose `aria-label`, icon, `disabled` and handler
  all switch on `activeSideChatId === null`; two icons with one hidden would leave both names in the
  accessibility tree.
- The Modal's confirm handler needs the pending id: read `state.sideChatDeleteConfirm` at click time
  and pass it to `deleteSideChat`; the effect clears the slot itself on both outcomes.
- The `Alert` is for the **action** error only — the pane's existing turn / load error surfaces are
  untouched.
- Keep the composer hint to one `Text` — no `Badge` state machine, no second gate.
