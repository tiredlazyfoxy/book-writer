# 018.chapter-history-variants — Chapter variants & history
<!-- roadmap:start -->
- **Stage:** 6.archive-history · **Track:** multi-step · **Size:** M/L
- **Delivers:** FEAT-014
- **Depends on:** `015.chapter-writing-free-mode`, `010.working-page`

## Definition
Surface a chapter's change history through the Variants navigator entry:
list a chapter's un-applied changes (variants) and its prior revisions,
compare any two, and apply one (apply takes the ordinary write path).
Show a historical body in the content pane; expose chapter versions to the
assistant (a versions-access tool).

## Scope
**In:** UC-058/059/060; US-062/063/064. Variants list; diff/compare;
apply-as-write; content-pane display of a revision.
**Out:** merge of a stale-base variant (refused; merge mechanics
post-MVP); the FEAT-016 consistency check the apply triggers is that
feature's, not this one's.

  — design during planning (see `011.chat-panel`).
